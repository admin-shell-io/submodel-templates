# Fire Protection on Railway Vehicles (Version 1.0)

This is a Submodel template specification for the Asset Administration Shell.

## Scope of the Submodel

The submodel Fire Protection on Railway Vehicles template provides a framework for creating the information required for fire protection on railway vehicles in accordance with the underlying standard.

Fire protection certificates are essential elements of the safety documentation for railway vehicles. These certificates are based on fire test and assessment reports according to EN 45545-2 [7] and are issued for individual materials or components, or for defined groupings of components.

Having such submodel enables harmonized data exchange between suppliers and railway manufacturers, while ensuring efficient maintenance and updating of fire protection data throughout its entire life cycle.
