# Equipment Under Control (Version 1.0)

This is a Submodel template specification for the Asset Administration Shell.

## Scope of the Submodel

Equipment Under Control (EUC) is the equipment, machinery, apparatus or plant used for manufacturing, process, transportation, medical or other activities. Where a risk analysis shows that the risk arising from the EUC is not acceptable, risk reduction is achieved by safety measures, among them the safety instrumented functions specified in IEC 61511.

This Submodel aims to provide a standardized and machine-readable way to exchange the description of the equipment under control and of the hazards associated with it, so that the basis on which safety integrity levels were allocated can be exchanged and reviewed rather than remaining in risk analysis documents.

The Submodel covers the following subject areas:

- **EUC specification**: the description and boundary of the equipment under control, and its control system
- **Hazardous events**: the hazardous events identified for the equipment, their causes and the independent protection layers credited against them
- **Hazards from combined safe process states**: hazards that arise when individual process states, each safe in isolation, occur together, and the measures taken to avoid them
- **Supporting reports**: references to the SIL allocation report and the hazard and risk assessment report

The safety instrumented functions that reduce the risk of the equipment under control are described by the Safety Instrumented Function Submodel.
